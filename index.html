How to play

  1. Drag & dropPlace the three blocks anywhere they fit on the 8×8 board.
  2. Fill a lineComplete a full row or column and it clears away.
  3. Chain combosClear lines back-to-back, or several at once, for huge points.
  4. Plan aheadThe game ends when none of your blocks fit. How far can you go?

Gameplay

How do I play?

Drag the three blocks at the bottom onto the board. Fill a complete row or column to clear it. Clearing several lines at once, or on back-to-back turns, builds combos worth extra points. The game ends when none of your blocks fit anywhere.

How do I earn coins?

You earn coins by clearing lines (more for combos) and a bonus for emptying the whole board. You can also tap 🎬 Free coins to watch an optional short video for +25 coins, or buy coin packs in the Shop.

What do the power-ups do?

Undo takes back your last move. Bomb clears a 3×3 area you tap. Shuffle swaps your remaining blocks for new ones.

Can I continue after the game ends?

Yes, once per game. Tap Watch ad to continue on the game-over screen to clear space in the middle of the board and keep going.

Purchases

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Remove Ads turns off the full-screen ads between games. Optional reward videos (free coins and continue) stay available because you choose to watch them. If full-screen ads still appear, open the Shop (tap your coin balance) and tap Restore purchases.

I got a new phone or reinstalled the game

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Remove Ads will come back.
Coins are stored only on your device, so they can't be restored after uninstalling.

My coin purchase didn't arrive

First close and reopen the game. If the coins still aren't there, email us with the date and your order number (from your Apple or Google receipt email) and we'll sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested through them:
• iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com
• Android: Google Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history, or Google Play refund request

Ads & privacy

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

On the game-over screen, tap Privacy options. On iPhone you can also change tracking permission in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.

An ad was inappropriate or broken

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Email us the time it happened and a short description (a screenshot helps) and we'll report and block it.

Contact us

Email keptappointment@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your phone model and whether you're on iPhone or Android.

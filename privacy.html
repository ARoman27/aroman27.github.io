Privacy Policy

Block Drop · Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the Block Drop mobile game ("the App"), published by YOUR NAME ("we"), collects and how it is used.

Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email, or any account. Your scores, best score and coin balance are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google and its partners may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewarded ads completed) and crash or performance data related to ads

Google's use of this data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. To verify purchases and restore them on a new device, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, the products you bought, purchase dates and prices, and your device's store receipt. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): where required by law, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time via "Privacy options" on the game-over screen.
  • iOS tracking: on iPhone you are asked whether to allow tracking. If you decline, ads are not personalised using your IDFA. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads).
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all game data stored on your device.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected by the advertising SDK is shared with Google and its advertising partners solely for the purposes described above.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.

Contact

Questions or requests: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com. See also our Support page.
